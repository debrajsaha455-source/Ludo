# 🎲 Ludo Game

This is a classic Ludo game project built entirely with **HTML**, **CSS**, and **Vanilla JavaScript**. It can be played smoothly right in your browser without any hassle.

  [![Play Online](https://img.shields.io/badge/Play_Online-blue?style=for-the-badge&logo=google-chrome)](https://debrajsaha455-source.github.io/Ludo/)

---

## ✨ Features

*   **Single-Player Mode:** You (🔴 Red) play against 3 automated bots (🟢 Green, 🟡 Yellow, 🔵 Blue).
*   **Interactive Dice:** Click to roll the dice, featuring visual dice faces (⚀ to ⚅).
*   **Smart Game Logic:** 
    *   Roll a 6 to bring a token out of the base.
    *   Get an extra turn for rolling a 6 or capturing an opponent's token.
*   **Safe Zones:** Tokens are safe and cannot be captured on ★ marked squares.
*   **Animations & UI:** Bouncing animations for tokens that are eligible to move, making it easy to know your options.
*   **Live Game Log:** Real-time messages on the screen showing what to do next or who captured whom.
*   **Responsive Design:** The game board adapts perfectly to all devices, from mobile phones to large desktop screens.
*   **New Game:** A dedicated button to easily restart the game at any time.

---

## 📜 How to Play

1. Click the **"Roll Dice"** button to roll the dice when the game starts.
2. You must roll a **6** to bring a token out of your base.
3. Click on the token you want to move during your turn.
4. If your token lands exactly on an opponent's token, the opponent's token is captured and sent back to its base.
5. The first player to get all 4 of their tokens to the center of the board wins the game!

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

*   **HTML5:** Used for building the main structure of the game.
*   **CSS3:** Used for the board design, grid layout, colors, and animations.
*   **JavaScript:** Used for controlling the dice rolls, bot movements, game logic, and DOM manipulation.
